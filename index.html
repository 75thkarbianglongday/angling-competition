📜 Rules and Regulations

1. Eligibility and Registration

  • The Angling Competition shall be open to all interested participants.
  • No entry fee shall be charged from any participant.
  • All participants must register their names online only before commencement of the competition.
  • Each participant shall be allowed only one fishing position, allotted by the Organising Committee.
  • Participants below 18 years of age must be accompanied by a parent or guardian.

2. Rules of Angling

  • Each participant shall be permitted to use one fishing rod with hook only.
  • Participants shall bring their own fishing rods, hooks, lines, floats and baits.
  • Angling shall be permitted only within the designated fishing area.
  • Fishing before the official starting signal or after the closing signal is prohibited.
  • Participants shall not disturb or interfere with other competitors.

3. Duration of Competition

  • The competition shall commence from 9:00 AM and conclude at 1:00 PM.
  • A common starting and closing signal shall be given to all participants.
  • Fish caught after the closing signal shall not be considered for evaluation.

4. Evaluation and Prize Distribution

  • The competition shall be judged on the basis of the single biggest fish caught by each participant during the prescribed competition period.
  • All fish caught must be presented to the designated officials for weighing and recording, and the fish will be released back to the pond.
  • In case of a tie, the participant catching the highest number of fish during the competition period shall be given preference. Any further tie shall be decided by the Organising Committee.

5. Conservation and Environmental Protection

  • Participants shall maintain cleanliness in and around the pond.
  • Disposal of plastic materials, fishing lines, hooks and other waste into the pond is strictly prohibited.
